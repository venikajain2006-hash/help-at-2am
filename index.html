Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I feel unsafe at night?

Move to a safer, well-lit place if possible and contact verified campus security or local emergency services.

What if I need medical assistance?

Contact the verified campus health centre or local emergency medical services for urgent needs.

Is this website an emergency service?

No. It is an academic support prototype that provides information and guidance only.

About This Project

Help at 2 A.M. is a student design-thinking project focused on making campus support information easier to find during late-night situations.

Our goal is to improve awareness, accessibility and confidence in finding the right support.
